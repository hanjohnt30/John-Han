02 / A little more

The story so far.

For all four years of high school, I’ve been part of FRC Team 604 Quixilver, where I’ve developed a passion for building, iterating, and working as a team. In 2024, our team won the FIRST Championship Milstein Division. Outside the shop, I played four years of football for the Leland Chargers and helped win the 2024 CCS Division V Championship as a starting offensive lineman—experiences that sharpened my discipline, resilience, and appreciation for performance under pressure.
