03 / A little more

The story so far.

I got into engineering because I wanted to build things that could do more than look impressive. I wanted to see what they were capable of.

Four years of competitive robotics taught me how to turn ideas into machines, push designs beyond the drawing board, and solve problems when things don't go as planned. From designing in CAD to competing on the drive team, I learned that a great design isn't just about how it works in theory, but how it performs when it matters.

Football reinforced that mindset. Four years on the offensive line taught me that strength alone means nothing without precision, preparation, and execution. Every play was a test of how well I could perform under pressure.

Now, as a mechanical engineering student at Purdue, I'm taking those lessons into bigger challenges. I want to design machines that are pushed to their limits, build systems that deliver under pressure, and create things that prove themselves through performance.
